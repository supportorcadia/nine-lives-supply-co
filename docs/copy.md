## Girth page (/girth)

**h1:** How to measure a cat that won't be measured.
**Body:**
You cannot ask a cat to stand still with a tape round its ribs, so don't. Wait until it's asleep on you. Run a soft tape round the chest just behind the front legs, at the widest point, snug but not pulling. Read it. Do it again the next time it's asleep, because the first number is usually wrong. If the two numbers are different, use the bigger one.

That number is the girth, and it's the only number the harness cares about. Weight doesn't help; two cats the same weight can be four centimetres apart round the chest.

**Size table (the one above).**
**Rule:** Between sizes, buy the smaller one. A harness that is a bit snug holds. A harness that is a bit loose is in a hedge.
**Fit check:** Two fingers flat under the strap at the back. Not three.
**No tape?** A shoelace and a ruler. Mark the lace, lay it flat.
**Closing line:** If you've measured twice and it still doesn't fit, send it back and we'll swap it. Postage on us if you tell us the girth you measured.

## From Kirkwall (/from-kirkwall) — shipping and returns

**h1:** Posted from Kirkwall.
**Shipping:**
Everything leaves from one place, which is Kirkwall, Orkney. That means it goes on a boat or a plane before it gets anywhere else, and we'd rather say so here than argue about it later.

- Mainland UK: £3.95, or free over £60. Two to three working days after it leaves us.
- Highlands, Islands, Northern Ireland: same price. Three to five working days, and longer if the boat isn't running, which happens.
- Engraved tags (03, 04, and kits 23 and 24) are cut after you order. Add three working days before dispatch. You'll see this in the basket before you pay, and on the confirmation, and on the tag.
- We post Monday to Friday. An order after two on a Friday goes Monday.
- UK only. Pounds only. If you're outside the UK we can't help, sorry.

**Returns:**
Thirty days, unused, in the kraft box it came in, and we'll refund it or swap it.

Harnesses: if it's the wrong size we'll swap it, no argument. Tell us the girth you measured and we'll pay the return postage, because then it's our fault or the tape's. If you didn't measure, we'll still swap it, but the postage is yours.

Engraved tags: we can't take these back unless we cut it wrong, because a tag with your number on it is no use to anybody else. Check the number before you pay. We check it too.

The shelter: if it hasn't been outside, thirty days like everything else. If it has, it's been used, and we can't resell it. If it's failed, that's different: email us a photograph and we'll sort it.

Anything faulty: photograph, email, and we'll replace it. Twelve months.

**Trade:** We supply two vets and a farm shop on the islands. If that's you, email; the site isn't built for it.

## Made in Orkney (/made-in-orkney)

**h1:** Made for a cat that lives here.
**Body:**
Most of what a pet shop sells is designed for a cat that lives in a first-floor flat in Guildford. Ours live outside, in horizontal rain, on single-track roads with no streetlights and no pavements, and they come home when they feel like it. From November to February it's dark by half past three. We started making collars because the ones you could buy either weren't reflective enough to be seen from a car or didn't break away when they caught on a fence, and we kept going.

Everything is sewn, cut and boxed on Orkney. The webbing is reflective through the weave, not printed on. The tags are engraved on a machine in the back room. The shelters are assembled here and go out in a kraft box with the stencil on the lid and one hi-vis sticker, which is the whole of our packaging.

We make five colourways: Slate, Moss, Ember, Sand and Hi-Vis. Hi-Vis outsells the other four combined, which is the right outcome, and it's the one we'll put in your basket unless you change it.

We don't put a puffin on anything.
