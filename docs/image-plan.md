# Image plan — Nine Lives Supply Co.

Every image is generated through ChatGPT in the browser from these prompts. Filenames live in /public/images (hero poster in /public/media). Prompt template, in order: subject and what it is doing; framing and crop as the aspect ratio; light; backdrop; lens and distance; grade; the Imagery DNA paragraph verbatim; the exclusions. Only the subject block changes.
